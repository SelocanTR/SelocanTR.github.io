Our app

Nomad Budget

Nomad Budget is an expense tracker app for digital nomads, expats and long-term travellers: one wallet per country in its own currency, one total in yours, and your journey on a 3D globe.

  • A wallet for every country, each in its own currency, and one total in the currency you choose
  • Autopilot turns Apple Pay payments and bank texts into drafts you confirm with one tap
  • Your route on a spinning globe, and what each country actually cost you
  • Compare your own spending pattern in another country before you move
